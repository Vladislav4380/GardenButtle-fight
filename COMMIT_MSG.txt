6×6 sounds: every hero has its own blow; moving and events are silent

- Each of the 12 heroes has its own blow (cue hero-<id>, 2–3 takes) from CC0
  packs in art/sfx-cc0/heroes, chosen by kind and closeness to a reference:
  swords ring of metal (beet: swing with a ring, peach: heavier clash), clubs a
  little duller (plank and wood hits, the potato's pick on stone), the onion's
  arrow says "вжих", the eggplant's magic says "фух" like the pepper's fire.
  The pepper's fire is short and quieter. SOURCES.md lists each file's pack and
  what was mixed.
- All blows are quiet with the low end cut (below 150 Hz); the bow keeps its
  string. Steps, sprints, the leap, abilities, falls, other shots, the round's
  end and the result are silent; the weapon-wide sounds and unused sources are
  gone.
- tools/build-sfx.cjs builds the hero cues; tools/import-local-sfx.cjs builds a
  local, git-ignored reference set (sfx-local, also src/assets/audio/source is
  ignored). The sound test page has a button per hero.
