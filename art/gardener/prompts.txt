The uploaded image is my game character: a chibi farmer with a straw hat, a short green cape, a leather apron,
gloves, boots and a garden hoe in the right hand.
Draw ONE sprite sheet of this character for a mobile farm game: a grid of 5 rows × 8 columns, 40 figures in total.
All figures are the same character, the same costume, the hoe in the same hand, drawn at EXACTLY the same size:
the same head width and the same total height in every cell (difference not more than 3%). In every row all figures
stand on one common ground line. Camera slightly from above, about 35 degrees down, the same camera in every cell.

ROWS = directions (the whole row keeps one direction):
row 1 - facing the camera (front);
row 2 - three-quarter front, turned to the RIGHT;
row 3 - side view, facing RIGHT;
row 4 - three-quarter back, turned to the RIGHT, no face visible;
row 5 - facing away from the camera (back), no face visible.

COLUMNS = poses (the same pose in the whole column):
column 1 - idle, relaxed, the hoe resting on the shoulder;
columns 2-5 - walk cycle in the row's direction: 2 contact step (one leg forward), 3 passing (other knee bent,
boot slightly lifted), 4 contact step with the other leg forward, 5 passing with the other leg;
column 6 - work wind-up: the hoe raised high with both hands, in the facing direction;
column 7 - work strike: the hoe digs into the ground in front of him, small clods of dark soil fly up;
column 8 - harvest: bending down, holding a small basket with green leaves in both hands.

Every figure stays inside its own cell with clear white space around it; figures must not touch or overlap.
White background, no grid lines, no shadows on the ground, no text, no numbers, no labels.
