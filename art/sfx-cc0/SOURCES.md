# Battle sound sources

Every file here is CC0 (public domain): free to use anywhere, credit not required.
`node tools/build-sfx.cjs` cuts them into `src/assets/audio/sfx`.

Each hero has its own blow (`heroes/<hero>_<n>`). They were chosen by kind and then, among those, by how close their sound is to a reference the user liked; nothing of the reference itself is used. Swords ring of metal, clubs are a little duller but not dead, the arrow says "вжих", magic says "фух" like fire.
